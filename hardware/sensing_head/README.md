# Sensing head: print sheet

Underwater chlorophyll sensor for the bloom buoy. All sizes in **mm** (the STL files are in mm).

| File | Print | Size | Filament |
|---|---|---|---|
| `head.stl` | 1 (main design: LED and sensor tilted 25° toward each other) | 62 × 62 × 45 | ~69 g |
| `head_straight.stl` | 1 (comparison: both straight) | 62 × 62 × 45 | ~69 g |
| `lid.stl` | 2 (one per head) | 62 × 62 × 12 | ~19 g |
| `shroud.stl` | 1 (both heads use it) | 68.6 × 68.6 × 32 | ~24 g |

Total about 200 g.

## Settings

- **Filament: black PETG.** Black so no light leaks through the walls; PETG because it sits in water.
  PLA is fine for a first fit check, not for long soaks.
- 0.2 mm layers, **4 or more walls**, **100% infill for the heads** (they must be waterproof),
  20% is fine for the lid and shroud.
- **Orientation:** each file is already flat on the bed as exported. Do not rotate.
  - head: the wide flat face with the round recess goes **down**.
  - lid: flat plate down, skirt up.
  - shroud: feet down.
- **Supports:** only under the round 3 mm glass recess on the bottom of each head
  ("support on build plate only" or a support blocker everywhere else). The angled holes print
  without supports.
- Holes print slightly small. If the 5 mm LED does not push into its hole, open it with a 5.2 mm drill.

## After printing (Vihaan)

1. Soak-test each head with no electronics for 1 hour. If water gets in, brush epoxy or
   silicone on the outside and test again.
2. Glass disc into the bottom recess with a bead of aquarium silicone; let it cure 24 h.
3. Red filter in the small square recess; light sensor chip-down on top (its plugs rest in the pocket,
   cables out through the end notches). Tape over the board's green power LED.
4. LED into the angled hole, tip against the glass. PG7 gland in the lid, cable through it.
5. Two M3 × 8 mm screws through the shroud's side holes into the head.

The design is generated by `make_head.py` (change a number there and rerun; never edit the STLs).
