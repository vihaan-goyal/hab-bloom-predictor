Alerter electronics: full parts list

Vihaan Goyal & Olivia Breiner · CSEF / ISEF 2027 · harmful algal bloom project
What it is: an Arduino "alerter" for a test tank. Each day it gets a bloom forecast plus the tank's chlorophyll, temperature and pH, decides when to switch a treatment ON/OFF, and shows it with LEDs, a buzzer and a relay. The logic, LEDs, buzzer, relay and laptop link already work. The Uno's analog header (A0-A5) broke off.
