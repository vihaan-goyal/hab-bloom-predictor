## Open points
- **Tank size mismatch:** arm A is shared, but seaweed tanks are 4-10 L and shellfish tanks 20 L. Either make all 21 tanks 20 L (then seaweed needs ~40 g kelp per tank, ~360 g total, more than the 100 g asked for) or give seaweed its own 3 untreated tanks (24 tanks).
- **Holding-tank food:** *Isochrysis* isn't on the NCMA request yet. Either add it or buy a commercial shellfish feed.
