## Notes
- **Winter to spring drawdown:** nitrate and silicate build up in winter, and the spring diatom bloom uses up about 90% of them by March-April. The tank bloom pulse copies that drop (`WATER_RECIPE.md`).
- **Summer:** warmer water, lower pH and lower dissolved oxygen, lowest in the West (the hypoxia region). Surface values are shown; bottom-water oxygen is lower still.
- **Micromolar conversions:** N mg/L × 71.4, P mg/L × 32.3, SiO₂ mg/L × 16.6.
