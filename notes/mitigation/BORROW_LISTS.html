Chemistry lab: items to borrow

Vihaan Goyal & Olivia Breiner · CSEF / ISEF 2027 project · needed late October to December 2026
The project: a model that forecasts harmful algal blooms in Long Island Sound, plus a bench test of gentle ways to stop a bloom early (live seaweed, filter-feeding shellfish, aeration), switched on only when the forecast warns. Tests run in small tanks and flasks with non-toxic algae cultures.
Would you be willing to be our Designated Supervisor? The science fair requires one to sign our forms before any hands-on work. We have a full plan and safety list to show you.

Robotics lab: items to borrow

Vihaan Goyal & Olivia Breiner · CSEF / ISEF 2027 project · needed October to December 2026
The project: an Arduino "alerter" that takes a daily bloom forecast and a tank's sensor readings, decides when to switch a treatment on and off, and drives a relay (pump) or servo (lowers a seaweed panel into the tank). The logic, LEDs, buzzer and relay already work on an Arduino Uno.

☐MultimeterChecking the 12 V supply, relay pins, wiring

☐6-pin female header strip (+ desoldering wick)Our Uno's analog-pin header (A0-A5) broke off; re-solder a new one
